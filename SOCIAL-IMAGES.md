## Two standing rules

**Careful with extreme close-ups of print.** The tiny signature marks — the
`SAM.B` and `ANOTHER PUNK` that sit inside the artwork — are garbled in some
images. Invisible at normal size, obvious in a macro. `01-bats-chest-flash`
is the clear example. `11-macro-rawhem-ink` is fine because it contains no
text at all. Check any macro before posting.

**The folder is not a safe source.** It holds every mockup ever generated,
including the rejected ones. The site's catalogue is the vetted set; anything
outside it needs a look first.
