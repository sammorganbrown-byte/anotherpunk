what is actually printed where. No flat lay goes in the repo.

## 1. Back shots — 14 of 17 products have none

Have a true back: **Westwood 69 Pink**, **Westwood 69 Black**, **Bodysuit**.

Three more have a *turn*, not a back, and should not be counted as one:
`63-saucer-turn`, `08-bwwhite-midturn`, `76-leopard-midturn`.

### The thing to decide before briefing any of them

**Most of these backs are blank.** Confirmed from the flats:

| Blank | What the back actually shows |
|---|---|
| Snow Wash Raw-Hem Boxy (the 5 tees) | Nothing. Plain washed black, distressed hem |
| Snow Washed Oversized (AP, Saucer Ov.) | Nothing on the back. Red ANOTHER PUNK on the LEFT SLEEVE |
| Staple white / black | Nothing. One small mark on the sleeve |

So a back shot of Bat Country, Tongue Box, The Jesus, Surrender Dorothy and
Saucer is **the same photograph five times** — an unprinted black shirt from
behind. Accurate, and worth almost nothing to a customer choosing between
them.

Two honest ways forward:

- **A. Shoot the plain backs anyway.** 9 distinct images (below), because five
  of them would be interchangeable. Cheapest, and it does answer "what does the
  back look like" for anyone who wonders.
- **B. Put a print on the back first.** This is what "even if new print" points
  at. It needs artwork from Sam per design, and then each back shot is worth
  having and worth 14 separate images. It also gives the field 14 new frames
  that are not another chest.

**B is the better product decision and the more expensive one.** A is not
wrong. Do not mix them: a range where five shirts have designed backs and five
have blank ones reads as unfinished.

## Accuracy rules for the brief

These are the ones that have gone wrong before, all recorded in
SOCIAL-IMAGES.md:

1. **A garment reference is not an artwork reference.** Supply both, or the
   model invents letterforms. "ANOT PUI" happened this way.
2. **Never dictate a spelling not verified against the source artwork**, and
   never defer to a cropped reference.
3. **Printed directly onto the cotton.** Say it explicitly, or a sleeve mark
   comes back as a sewn-on rectangular patch with stitching.
4. **The back of the snow-washed oversized carries the red ANOTHER PUNK on the
   LEFT SLEEVE.** It is visible in a back shot and is the only colour in it.
5. **No flat lays.** On-body, in the old-camera-flash look.
6. Raw hem is distressed and uneven, not a clean cut line. See the flats.
